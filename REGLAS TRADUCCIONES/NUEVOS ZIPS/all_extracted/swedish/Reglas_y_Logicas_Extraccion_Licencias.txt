# DOCUMENTACION DE REGLAS Y LOGICAS DE EXTRACCION / TRADUCCION DE LICENCIAS DE CONDUCIR (SWEDISH FULL)

================================================================================
1. CONTEXTO
================================================================================
- Pais y Tipo de Licencias: Licencias de conducir europeas / suecas y multilingues (formato de la UE con puntos numerados 1 al 12).
- Idioma de Origen: Sueco / Frances / Aleman / Espanol / Multilingue.
- Idioma Destino de Extraccion: INGLÉS (todos los valores de texto traducidos deben estar en ingles, a excepcion del campo "language" que especifica los idiomas originales del documento).

================================================================================
2. CAMPOS EXACTOS (ESTRUCTURA DE DATOS JSON)
================================================================================
- surname: Apellidos (Punto 1 del anverso).
- firstName: Unicamente el primer nombre del titular (extraido del Punto 2).
- middleName: Todos los nombres secundarios restantes (Punto 2).
- firstNames: Todos los nombres de pila unidos.
- fullName: Nombre completo concatenado (surname + firstName + middleName).
- licenseNumber: Numero principal de licencia (Punto 5). Solo caracteres alfanumericos.
- assignedNumber: Numero correlativo interno/referencia de expediente asignado por el usuario (ej. B1758).
- dateOfBirth: Fecha de nacimiento (Punto 3). Formato: DD Month YYYY.
- placeOfBirth: Lugar de nacimiento (Punto 3). Traducido al ingles.
- issueDate: Fecha de expedicion (Punto 4a). Formato: DD Month YYYY.
- expiryDate: Fecha de caducidad/expiracion (Punto 4b). Formato: DD Month YYYY.
- authority: Autoridad emisora (Punto 4c). Traducido al ingles + Nombre del pais de origen en ingles al final.
- class: Lista de categorias de vehiculos autorizadas (Punto 9 del anverso), separadas por comas.
- classDescriptions: Mapeo detallado de descripciones oficiales en ingles para cada categoria autorizada.
- categoriesDates: Matriz de fechas de validez por categoria (Tabla del reverso, Columna 10). Agrupado por fecha identica.
- codes: Codigo administrativo de referencia (Punto 4d del anverso). Solo valor alfanumerico.
- explicacionCodigos: Explicacion formal de restricciones y codigos de observacion (Columna 12 del reverso / anverso).
- code106From: Fecha de inicio para la restriccion/codigo 106 (si aplica).
- code106To: Fecha de fin/caducidad para la restriccion/codigo 106 (si aplica).
- language: Idioma(s) original(es) identificado(s) en la licencia (ej. "Swedish", "French").

================================================================================
3. REGLAS DE TRADUCCION Y FORMATO
================================================================================
1. Formato de Fechas:
   Todas las fechas (dateOfBirth, issueDate, expiryDate, code106From, code106To) DEBEN ser transformadas al formato exacto: "DD Month YYYY" (ejemplo: 01 January 2000, 15 August 2025).

2. Division de Nombres:
   - firstName: Contiene exclusivamente la primera palabra del listado de nombres (Punto 2).
   - middleName: Contiene el resto de los nombres dados. Si solo hay un nombre, permanecerá vacio.

3. Traduccion de Ubicacion y Autoridad:
   - placeOfBirth: Se extrae del Punto 3 y se traduce su denominacion oficial al ingles.
   - authority: Se extrae la entidad emisora del Punto 4c, se traduce al ingles y SIEMPRE se concatena el pais de origen en ingles al final (ej. "Transport Agency, Sweden").

4. Traduccion de Categorias (classDescriptions):
   Cada categoria de la lista "class" se mapea contra el siguiente catalogo estandarizado en ingles:
   - AM: AM - 2-wheel, 3-wheel vehicles and light quadricycles with a maximum design speed of not more than 45 km/h.
   - A1: A1 - light motorcycles with a cylinder capacity not more than 125 cubic centimetres and a power rating less than 11 kW.
   - A2: A2 - motorcycles with a power rating under 35 kW.
   - A: A - heavy motorcycles without power restrictions.
   - B: B - passenger vehicles weighing up to 3,500 kg and seating not more than eight passengers.
   - BE: BE - vehicle of category B towing a heavy trailer of under 3,500 kg.
   - B1: B1- quadricycles.
   - C1: C1 - goods vehicles between 3,500 kg and 7,500 kg and for up to eight passengers-
   - C1E: C1E - vehicle of category C1 or B towing a heavy trailer; with a combined mass of up to 12,000 kg.
   - C: C - goods vehicles weighing more than 3,500 kg and seating not more than eight passengers.
   - CE: CE - vehicle of category C towing a heavy trailer.
   - D1: D1 - passenger vehicles built for fewer than 16 passengers and that is no longer than 8 m.
   - D1E: D1E - vehicle of category D1 towing a heavy trailer.
   - D: D - passenger vehicles for more than eight passengers.
   - DE: DE - vehicle of category D towing a heavy trailer.
   - T: T-Tractor

5. Estructura de Fechas de Categorias (categoriesDates):
   - Se analiza la tabla del reverso (Columna 10).
   - Se agrupan las categorias que comparten la misma fecha exacta usando el formato: "Classes: Date".
   - REGLA CRITICA: Cada grupo DEBE ir obligatoriamente en su propia linea mediante un salto de linea (\n).
   Ejemplo:
   AM: 01 January 2000
   B1, B: 03 February 2001
   C1, C: 01 March 2002

6. Codigos de Observacion / Restricciones (explicacionCodigos):
   Se busca en la Columna 12 (Reverso) y restricciones del anverso. Se genera la redaccion oficial en ingles:
   - Codigo "70": "70. Exchange of driver's license number"
   - Codigo "01": "01- Correction of vision and/or eye protection"
   - Restriccion A1: "Class A1- limited to 45 km/h"
   - Codigo "106": "106- Operation of motor vehicles for the transport of passengers, with a maximum weight of 3,500 kilograms and more than 17 seats (including the driver’s seat)"

================================================================================
4. CASOS ESPECIALES Y EXCEPCIONES
================================================================================
- Ilegibilidad / Datos Faltantes: Si un campo opcional no esta presente, se asigna una cadena vacia "".
- Caracteres Limpios: En licenseNumber y codes (Punto 4d) se eliminan guiones o espacios innecesarios, conservando solo caracteres alfanumericos.
- Multiples Idiomas: El campo "language" registrara todos los idiomas presentes (ej. "Swedish and English").

================================================================================
5. SYSTEM PROMPT SUGERIDO PARA GEMINI API
================================================================================
You are an expert OCR and document translation system specialized in European driver's licenses (including Swedish and multilingual EU standards). Analyze the provided Front and Back images of the license.

IMPORTANT CONSTRAINTS:
1. ALL extracted values MUST be translated into English, EXCEPT for the "language" field, which specifies the original source language(s) on the card.
2. All dates MUST be formatted strictly as "DD Month YYYY" (e.g., "14 May 2018").
3. Return ONLY a valid JSON object strictly matching the provided JSON Schema.

EXTRACTION INSTRUCTIONS:
- "surname": Family name (Section 1).
- "firstName": Extract ONLY the very first given name (Section 2).
- "middleName": Extract ALL remaining given names after the first given name. Leave empty if none.
- "firstNames": All given names joined together.
- "licenseNumber": Main driver's license number (Section 5). Extract alphanumeric characters only.
- "dateOfBirth": Date of birth (Section 3).
- "placeOfBirth": Location of birth (Section 3), translated to English.
- "issueDate": Issue date (Section 4a).
- "expiryDate": Expiration date (Section 4b).
- "authority": Issuing authority (Section 4c), translated to English AND always append the country name in English at the end (e.g. "Transport Agency, Sweden").
- "class": All vehicle categories listed on the front (Section 9), separated by commas (e.g. "AM, B1, B").
- "categoriesDates": Analyze Column 10 of the table on the BACK. Group categories sharing the same issue date using the format "Classes: Date". Each group MUST be on its own line using a newline character (\n). Example:
  AM: 01 January 2000
  B1, B: 03 February 2001
- "codes": Administrative reference number from Section 4d on the front.
- "explicacionCodigos": Search Column 12 on the BACK and front restrictions. If matched, output the EXACT English text:
  * Code 70: "70. Exchange of driver's license number"
  * Code 01: "01- Correction of vision and/or eye protection"
  * Class A1 restriction: "Class A1- limited to 45 km/h"
  * Code 106: "106- Operation of motor vehicles for the transport of passengers, with a maximum weight of 3,500 kilograms and more than 17 seats (including the driver’s seat)"
  Combine multiple matches into a single paragraph. If none match, leave empty.
- "code106From" & "code106To": Extract validity start and end dates for code 106 if present.
- "language": Identify the original language(s) present on the document (e.g. "Swedish", "French").
