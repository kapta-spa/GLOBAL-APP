# DOCUMENTACIÓN DE REGLAS Y LÓGICA DE EXTRACCIÓN - NORWAY FULL / EU LICENSES

## 1. Contexto
- **País de Origen:** Licencias de conducir del Espacio Económico Europeo (Francia, Noruega, etc.).
- **Idioma de Entrada:** Francés / Noruego / Lenguas Oficiales UE.
- **Idioma de Salida (Traducción):** Inglés.
- **Propósito:** Extracción mediante visión multimodal (Gemini API) y poblamiento automatizado de plantillas de documentos Word (.docx).

## 3. Reglas de Traducción y Formato

1. **Idioma Global de Salida:** Todos los valores de texto devueltos deben estar estrictamente en **INGLÉS**.
2. **Formato de Fechas:** Todas las fechas se deben formatear como `DD Month YYYY` (ejemplo: `01 January 2000`, `15 March 2021`).
3. **Formato de `categoriesDates`:**
   - Agrupar categorías que comparten la misma fecha de validez.
   - Formato exacto: `CÓDIGO1, CÓDIGO2: DD Month YYYY`
   - **Regla estricta:** Cada grupo debe ir en una línea separada. NO poner todas las categorías en un solo renglón.
   - Ejemplo:
     AM: 01 January 2000
     B1, B: 03 February 2001
     C1, C: 01 March 2002
4. **Formato de `classDescriptions`:**
   - Mapear las categorías de la parte frontal con sus descripciones estándar en inglés:
     - `AM`: 2-wheel, 3-wheel vehicles and light quadricycles with a maximum design speed of not more than 45 km/h.
     - `A1`: light motorcycles with a cylinder capacity not more than 125 cubic centimetres and a power rating less than 11 kW.
     - `A2`: motorcycles with a power rating under 35 kW.
     - `A`: heavy motorcycles without power restrictions.
     - `B`: passenger vehicles weighing up to 3,500 kg and seating not more than eight passengers.
     - `BE`: vehicle of category B towing a heavy trailer of under 3,500 kg.
     - `B1`: quadricycles.
     - `C1`: goods vehicles between 3,500 kg and 7,500 kg and for up to eight passengers.
     - `C1E`: vehicle of category C1 or B towing a heavy trailer; with a combined mass of up to 12,000 kg.
     - `C`: goods vehicles weighing more than 3,500 kg and seating not more than eight passengers.
     - `CE`: vehicle of category C towing a heavy trailer.
     - `D1`: passenger vehicles built for fewer than 16 passengers and that is no longer than 8 m.
     - `D1E`: vehicle of category D1 towing a heavy trailer.
     - `D`: passenger vehicles for more than eight passengers.
     - `DE`: vehicle of category D towing a heavy trailer.
     - `M`: 2-wheel moped.
     - `T`: Tractor maximum design speeds of 40 km/h.
     - `S`: Snowmobile with or without a trailer sledge.

## 4. Casos Especiales y Excepciones

1. **Códigos 70 y 71:**
   - Si se detecta el código `70` o `71` en la columna 12 del reverso, extraer **ÚNICAMENTE** el número de licencia que le sigue (ejemplo: si aparece `71. 123456.ML`, el resultado debe ser `123456.ML`).
2. **Código 01 / 01.06:**
   - `01`: `"01- Vision correction and/or protection"`
   - `01.06`: `"01.06- Prescribed spectacles or contact lenses"`
3. **Código 106:**
   - Texto a generar: `"106- Subject to the application of article R. 413-5 during probationary period from [DateFrom] to [DateTo] (Speed limits: 110km/h in Freeways where limit is 130km/h, 100km/h in freeways with lower speed limit or double roads, 80 km/h on other roads.)"`
4. **Prohibición de Citas y Fuentes:**
   - **ESTRICTAMENTE PROHIBIDO** incluir textos de fuentes, enlaces de búsqueda o URLs externas (ej. `https://www.vegvesen.no...`). Extraer únicamente la información visible en el documento.

## 5. System Prompt Sugerido para la API de Gemini

You are an expert OCR and translation system for official driver's licenses. Analyze the provided front and back images of the driver's license.

CRITICAL INSTRUCTIONS:
1. ALL OUTPUT VALUES MUST BE IN ENGLISH.
2. DO NOT include any URLs, web citations, or external sources (e.g. no vegvesen.no links).
3. Format all dates strictly as "DD Month YYYY" (e.g., "01 January 2020").

EXTRACTION RULES:
- surname: Family name (Point 1).
- firstName: ONLY the first given name (Point 2).
- middleName: ALL remaining given names after the first (Point 2). Leave empty if only one name.
- licenseNumber: Point 5 identification number (alphanumeric only).
- assignedNumber: Point 4d personal reference number.
- dateOfBirth: Point 3 date.
- placeOfBirth: Point 3 location translated into English.
- issueDate: Point 4a date.
- expiryDate: Point 4b date.
- authority: Point 4c authority translated to English, appending country name in English at the end.
- class: All category codes listed on Front (Point 9), comma-separated.
- categoriesDates: Back table Column 10. Group categories with identical dates in format: "CODE1, CODE2: DD Month YYYY". Each date group MUST be on a NEW LINE.
- codes: Top-left corner of the back.
- explicacionCodigos: Column 12 (Back). Rules:
  - Code 70 or 71: Return ONLY the license number following the code (e.g. "123456.ML").
  - Code 01: "01- Vision correction and/or protection"
  - Code 01.06: "01.06- Prescribed spectacles or contact lenses"
  - Code 106: "106- Subject to the application of article R. 413-5 during probationary period from [DateFrom] to [DateTo] (Speed limits: 110km/h in Freeways where limit is 130km/h, 100km/h in freeways with lower speed limit or double roads, 80 km/h on other roads.)"
- classDescriptions: Standard English descriptions for each category in Point 9, formatted as "CODE - Description", one per line.

Return valid JSON adhering to the specified schema.
